Self-hosted clipping tool

The chat knows when something happened.
Clipper is listening.

BestStreamersMoments Clipper watches live Twitch chat, spots the moments it explodes, and cuts them into captioned vertical clips — ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Everything runs on your own machine, and nothing leaves it until you say so.

What it does

One operator, one machine, no cloud service in the middle.

📈

Chat-spike detection

Message rate is measured against a rolling baseline, with bots and repeated spam filtered out. When the rate multiplies, that's a moment.

⏪

Always-on buffer

A rolling recording buffer means the seconds before the reaction are already on disk. The punchline is never missed by a few seconds.

🗣️

Word-level captions

Audio is transcribed locally and burned in word by word, in several caption styles, with optional profanity masking.

📱

Vertical layouts

Seven 1080×1920 layouts — blurred background, webcam cut-in, chat panel, reaction split and more — rendered with hardware encoding.

🎛️

Local dashboard

A token-protected dashboard on your own loopback address: review clips, re-cut them, rewrite titles, approve or reject.

🔒

Approval before publishing

Public platforms are gated. A clip cannot reach YouTube, TikTok or Instagram until you explicitly approve that clip.

How it works

From a live stream to a clip waiting for your review.

  1. Watch

    Clipper joins the public chat of the channels you choose, read-only and anonymously, and records the live stream into a rolling buffer.

  2. Detect

    Message rate, reaction keywords and emote bursts are scored against the channel's own baseline. A real spike triggers a cut.

  3. Cut and transcribe

    The moment is cut from the buffer with lead-in and lead-out, then transcribed locally — audio never leaves your computer.

  4. Render

    A vertical clip is produced with captions, an optional hook intro, zoom and chat overlays, queued so one render runs at a time.

  5. Review and approve

    The clip appears in your dashboard as awaiting approval. Nothing is uploaded to a public platform before you approve that specific clip.

Platforms

Each integration uses the platform's official API and your own account. The tool follows each platform's rules rather than working around them.

▶

YouTube Shorts

Uploads through the YouTube Data API to a channel you verify in advance. Unverified API projects may only upload as private.

♪

TikTok

Content Posting API in Inbox Upload mode: the clip lands in your drafts and you finish the caption and publish it in the TikTok app yourself.

◎

Instagram Reels

Optional, through the Instagram Graph API with a Professional account and your own access token.

📤

Local delivery

Or skip platforms entirely: a QR code to your phone, a synced folder, or a webhook you point wherever you like.

About this project

BestStreamersMoments Clipper is a personal, self-hosted project, not a hosted service. There is no sign-up, no user account and no server that receives your clips.
